Scholastic Toy Safety Checker

A first-pass chemical safety screen for products before they're purchased — cross-referencing ingredient CAS numbers against Australian government reference lists, flagging hazardous substances, and stating whether a Safety Data Sheet is required.

This is an interactive preview with placeholder data — no real product, supplier, or company data is used.
